METHODOLOGY

How to read the data

Financial growth. Revenue and net profit YoY compare aggregate current-period values with reconstructed prior-year totals across currently classified A-share constituents. Figures are cumulative for each reporting period.

Positive companies. Counts show constituents whose reported YoY growth is above zero. The denominator excludes missing observations.

Price return. The Shenwan Level 3 industry index return during the reporting quarter is used as a stable proxy for average constituent price performance.

Classification. Historical figures use the latest available Shenwan Level 3 constituent map and may therefore contain survivorship or reclassification bias.
